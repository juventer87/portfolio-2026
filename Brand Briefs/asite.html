The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

ASITE is a web development and UI/UX lab. Its mark — a folded, dimensional 'A' in two blues with a clean geometric wordmark — signals precision and craft in digital product. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a sharp, modern identity that reads equally to engineers and to the brands they design for.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the folded-A mark and squared wordmark
  • Define a precise, technical-yet-human visual language
  • Establish a clear, confident product voice
  • Set colour and type for product, pitch and web

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

ASITE was founded on a simple frustration: too often, the people who design a site and the people who build it never share a room. ASITE is both — a lab where UI/UX and development sit together, so an interface is engineered as carefully as it is drawn. The folded 'A' is that idea made visual: a single ribbon that turns a flat letter into something structural, built.

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'A site' — the thing they make; the folded ribbon 'A' turns a flat letter into something structural and built.

Cultural resonance

In tech, a confident geometric mark signals competence and modernity to founders and CTOs alike.

Emotional recipe

Confidence and relief — the sense that the work is in capable hands.

Interpretation for the brand

Lead with the dimensional A and the squared type; keep UI clean, blue-led and generous with space.
