The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Sarafy is a fintech digital wallet. Its mark — a lightning-bolt 'S' split blue and violet over a squared wordmark — signals speed, security and modern finance. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a confident, trustworthy fintech identity for app, web and growth.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the lightning-S mark and squared wordmark
  • Define a secure, modern fintech visual language
  • Establish a confident, clear product voice
  • Set colour and type for app, web and growth

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Sarafy takes its name from the Arabic 'sarraf' — the trusted money-changer who has moved value for people for centuries. The brand brings that role into the phone: a wallet where money moves instantly and safely. The lightning-bolt 'S' captures the speed; the blue-to-violet gradient signals both the trust of finance and the energy of something new.

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Sarafy' — from the trusted money-changer ('sarraf'); the lightning 'S' signals instant movement.

Cultural resonance

The money-changer is a deeply trusted figure across the region — the brand modernises that trust.

Emotional recipe

Confidence, control and the ease of money that just works.

Interpretation for the brand
