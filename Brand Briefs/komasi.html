The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Komasi, under the 'Premiumflex' line, manufactures cutting and grinding discs. Its heavy wordmark — with a spinning disc forming the 'O' over a blue 'Premiumflex' bar — reads as industrial strength and reliability. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a tough, trustworthy B2B identity for packaging, trade and distribution.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the disc-in-O wordmark and Premiumflex lockup
  • Define a rugged, industrial visual language
  • Establish a confident, performance-led trade voice
  • Set colour and type for packaging, datasheets and trade

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Komasi makes the consumables that tradespeople and fabricators stake their work on — cutting and grinding discs that have to perform every single time. The 'Premiumflex' line promises both quality and the controlled flex a good disc needs. The disc spinning inside the 'O' turns the product into the logo: this is a brand that runs on reliability under load.

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Premiumflex' states the product promise; the disc-in-'O' makes the product the icon.

Cultural resonance

In the trades, brands earn loyalty through reliability — the look must signal industrial trust.

Emotional recipe

Confidence and reassurance — the disc will not let you down.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the wordmark heavy and the blue bar strong; build packaging that reads instantly on a busy trade shelf.
