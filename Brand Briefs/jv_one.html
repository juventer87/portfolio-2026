The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

JV One is a corporate venturing company first identified in 2012. The mark pairs a custom rounded wordmark with an eagle motif split into bold red wings and a steel reflection. Mohammed Vaseeuddin revisits the original brief here in the eight-section parameter, preserving the eagle, custom type and the red/grey rationale.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Preserve the eagle mark and custom alphabet
  • Restate the red/grey colour logic
  • Frame the venturing positioning clearly
  • Set colour and type for corporate applications

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

JV One was conceived in 2012 as a corporate venturing company — backing and building new business opportunities. The eagle was chosen deliberately: keen-eyed, ambitious and unafraid of height. The custom wordmark and the eagle's split wings became the brand's signature, and the original work paired a decisive red with a grounded grey to balance passion with judgement.

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'JV One' frames the venturing intent; the eagle gives ambition a fearless, far-seeing face.

Cultural resonance

The eagle is a near-universal symbol of vision and courage — apt for a backer of bold ideas.

Emotional recipe

Confidence and aspiration — the feeling of being believed in.

Interpretation for the brand

Hold the custom wordmark and split-wing eagle; let red carry passion and grey carry judgement, exactly as the original intended.
