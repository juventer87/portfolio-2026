The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Beekeeper's is a natural honey brand. Its badge already carries the strategy: a hexagon, a detailed bee, 'estd 2002', 'The Mark of Nature' and '100% Natural'. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a warm, trustworthy identity that lives up to the purity the mark already promises.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the hexagon badge and condensed wordmark
  • Define a natural, artisanal packaging language
  • Establish a warm, honest brand voice
  • Set colour and type for jars, retail and digital

01Discovery

Study the honey range, its sourcing and the natural-food shelf.

02Essence

03System

Badge usage, label architecture, palette and type.

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Beekeeper's was founded in 2002 by people who refused to cut their honey — built on the belief that the most trustworthy product is the one nature already finished. The hexagon is the honeycomb itself; the bee is the maker; and the surrounding promises ('The Mark of Nature', '100% Natural') turn the badge into a seal of trust before a word is read.

05

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Beekeeper's' centres the maker; the hexagon and bee signal honeycomb, craft and certification at once.

Cultural resonance

Honey is an ancient symbol of purity and care — the badge taps that instinctive trust.

Emotional recipe

Comfort, warmth and the reassurance of something genuinely natural.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the hexagon seal and the two promises; let honey amber lead, grounded by deep ink and an optional meadow green.
