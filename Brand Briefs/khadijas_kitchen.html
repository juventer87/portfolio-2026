The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Khadija's Kitchen is a home kitchen and catering brand. Its vivid magenta roundel hides a smile in the wordmark, signalling warmth, joy and home cooking. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a friendly, appetising identity ready for menus, packaging and social.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the smiling roundel and wordmark
  • Define a warm, appetising visual language
  • Establish a friendly, homely brand voice
  • Set colour and type for menus, packaging and digital

01Discovery

Understand the cuisine, the home-cook story and the catering offer.

02Essence

Frame the brand around joyful home cooking.

03System

Logo behaviour, palette, type and menu/packaging templates.

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Khadija's Kitchen carries a personal name into a personal promise: food made the way it is at home, with care and a little joy. Khadija — historically a respected businesswoman and provider — lends the brand warmth and trust. The hidden smile in the roundel says it plainly: this is food that's meant to make you happy.

05

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

A personal name signals a real cook behind the food; the hidden smile makes the promise of joy explicit.

Cultural resonance

Named home kitchens carry deep trust — you are eating someone's actual recipes.

Emotional recipe

Comfort, joy and the feeling of being fed by someone who cares.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the smile and the vivid pink; make everything feel hand-made, generous and happy.
