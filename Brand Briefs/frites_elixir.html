The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Frites & Elixir is a café-bistro built on a pairing: frites (comfort) and elixir (the crafted drink). Its elegant serif type wrapped around a sage-grey ampersand makes the '&' the hero. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a playful-premium hospitality identity that celebrates that pairing.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Make the ampersand the signature device
  • Define a warm, elevated bistro visual language
  • Establish a witty, characterful venue voice
  • Set colour and type for menus, signage and social

01Discovery

Understand the venue, its menu pairing and the guest it wants.

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Frites & Elixir was born from a simple, delightful contradiction: the humblest comfort food (frites) and the most crafted of drinks (an elixir), served side by side without apology. The oversized ampersand makes the union the whole point — this is a place built on pairings, where the everyday and the elevated share a table.

05

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Frites & Elixir' names the high-low pairing; the giant ampersand makes the union the brand's icon.

Cultural resonance

Bistro culture celebrates the pleasure of small indulgences shared — the '&' captures it.

Emotional recipe

Delight, ease and a little indulgence.

Interpretation for the brand

Let the ampersand lead as a graphic device; pair elegant serif type with a calm sage and a warm brass accent.
