The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Seema is the personal studio of an interior designer. The signature-style wordmark over a soft, watercolour texture positions the work as personal and tactile. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a calm, refined identity that puts the designer's hand and taste at the centre.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the handwritten wordmark into a dependable signature mark
  • Define a quiet, material-led visual language
  • Establish a warm, assured studio voice
  • Set colour and type for proposals, boards and social

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Seema means 'boundary' or 'edge' — the line that turns empty space into a considered room. The studio is named for the designer herself, and the handwritten mark keeps that personal promise visible: every project is signed, not stamped. The soft grey-green wash beneath it sets the studio's quiet, material temperament from the first glance.

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Seema' — boundary or defining edge; the handwritten signature frames the designer's personal authorship.

Cultural resonance

A signature signals craft and accountability — the designer stands behind every room.

Emotional recipe

Calm, warmth and belonging — the feeling of a space made just for you.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the mark hand-made and the world muted and material; let the work be the loudest thing.
