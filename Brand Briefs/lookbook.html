The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Lookbook is an online fashion-catalog service that brings designers into a single catalog. Its mark turns an open book into flowing pink pages beneath a bold wordmark. This brief restates the 2020 identity within the eight-section parameter, keeping the two-colour, book-led idea intact.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Preserve the open-book mark and bold wordmark
  • Hold a fashion-forward, two-colour visual language
  • Define a curious, stylish platform voice
  • Set colour and type for the platform, print and social

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Lookbook was built to solve a discovery problem: dozens of designers, no single place to browse them. The name says it instantly — a lookbook is where you go to see. The mark fuses an open book with fashion pages, a verbal idea made visual, and was deliberately held to two confident colours so it would read at any size and stand the test of time.

05

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Lookbook' is the fashion artefact itself; the open-book mark renders the name literally and elegantly.

Cultural resonance

The lookbook is a beloved fashion ritual — turning pages to find the next look.

Emotional recipe

Curiosity and the small thrill of discovering something to wear.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the two-colour book mark crisp; let the catalog imagery bring the colour and variety.
