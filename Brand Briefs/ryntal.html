The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Ryntal is a proptech rentals brand (a business plan is already on file). Its rounded amber wordmark with a house-and-'r' mark feels approachable and modern. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build a warm, trustworthy identity that makes renting feel simple and human, mapped onto the eight-section parameter.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the house-mark and rounded wordmark
  • Define a friendly, trustworthy product language
  • Establish a clear, reassuring rentals voice
  • Set colour and type for app, web and print

02

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Ryntal was built on a frustration the business plan names directly: renting is stressful, opaque and cold. Ryntal's answer is warmth and simplicity — a platform that treats renters and owners like people, not transactions. The house-and-'r' mark, set in a friendly amber, makes that promise visible from the first glance: this is a home brand, not a database.

05

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Ryntal' — a fresh spelling of rental; the house-and-'r' mark makes the home promise instant.

Cultural resonance

Warm, rounded amber stands apart in a category that usually defaults to cold corporate blue.

Emotional recipe

Relief, optimism and the feeling of being looked after.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the mark rounded and the amber bright; design product surfaces that feel calm and human.
