The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Rayhaan, 'The Frolic', is a travel-vlogger personal brand. Beneath the wordmark sits a row of line-icons — backpack, camp, smile, bridge/city, landscape — that map the creator's whole journey. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to turn that icon set into a flexible content brand for thumbnails, channels and merch.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Refine the wordmark and the five-icon system
  • Build a flexible identity for thumbnails and channels
  • Define an upbeat, wanderer's voice
  • Set colour and type for video, web and merch

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Rayhaan — 'The Frolic' — is the brand of a traveller who treats the world as a playground: a bag always packed, a tent for anywhere, a smile as standard kit, and a love of roads, bridges and the cities they lead to. The name itself means a fragrant, comforting herb — the freshness of being on the move. The five icons aren't decoration; they're the creed: stop anywhere and camp, get closer to nature, keep smiling, follow the road.

05

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Rayhaan' — a fragrant, comforting herb; 'The Frolic' frames travel as play, and the five icons map the journey.

Cultural resonance

Travel creators build trust through authenticity and joy — the icon creed signals exactly that.

Emotional recipe

Freedom, optimism and wanderlust — the itch to pack a bag.

Interpretation for the brand

Treat the five icons as a living library; keep the wordmark clean and let an adventurous, outdoor accent palette carry the energy.
