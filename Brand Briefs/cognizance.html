The Introduction

About the project · our role · our approach

About the Project

Cognizance is the newsletter of Unitech — a recurring publication that keeps the organisation informed and connected. The circular 'C' roundel and bold condensed wordmark give it the confidence of a masthead. Mohammed Vaseeuddin was asked to build an identity that feels editorial and credible while sitting neatly under the Unitech parent.

Our Role — Mohammed Vaseeuddin

  • Establish the roundel and wordmark as a repeatable masthead
  • Define a section system for recurring issues
  • Set a clear, engaging internal-comms voice
  • Define colour and type that flex from print to inbox

Brand Origin Story

History & heritage

Cognizance means awareness — knowing what is happening and why it matters. Born inside Unitech, the newsletter exists to turn scattered updates into shared understanding across the organisation. The blue 'C' roundel gives that idea a confident, editorial face; the condensed wordmark gives every issue the authority of a real masthead, not a memo.

Brand Essence

Meaning · culture · interpretation

Name & symbol

'Cognizance' — awareness, knowing; the 'C' roundel and masthead give internal knowledge an editorial identity.

Cultural resonance

A well-branded internal publication signals an organisation that values its people's attention.

Emotional recipe

Belonging and informed confidence — feeling in the loop.

Interpretation for the brand

Keep the roundel and condensed type masthead-strong; colour-code sections and respect the Unitech endorsement.
