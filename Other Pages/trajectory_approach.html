Trajectory.

Read this part as the résumé. Sixteen years, one hub, and a brief that reset with every market it reached.

i. Geography

The hub never moved — Dubai, for the better part of sixteen years — but the work did. It reached into Saudi Arabia, the United Kingdom, Pakistan, India, and the United States. What reads as premium in Riyadh, what clears procurement in Sharjah, and what a diaspora audience trusts in Karachi or Houston are not the same problem wearing different clothes. Each market reset the brief. Each one sharpened a different muscle.

ii. Sectors

The industries shifted as deliberately as the geography. Industrial building materials and fasteners, passive fire protection, LED and media-façade technology, cosmetics, hospitality and specialty food, organic consumer goods, sport, and independent publishing. Different regulations, different buyers, different rituals — the same discipline underneath. Build the brand and the business case end to end, and carry the complexity so the client never has to.

iii. Rooms

The rooms varied just as widely. Founder-led consumer brands where the strategist also names the product and sets the palette. Family conglomerates and diversified groups where a single decision moves through three generations before it ships. Manufacturers and distributors crossing a border for the first time, staking a year of margin on a market they have never sold into. Moving across that range — creative one week, the financial model the next — is the part that compounds.

Approach.

The work answers to two masters and refuses to choose between them.

i. The thesis

Nothing ships on taste alone; nothing ships on data alone. A brand has to survive the spreadsheet, and a strategy has to survive first contact with how people actually feel. The point where the two break even is where the work is allowed to leave the studio — reason and resonance, costed and earned in the same decision.

ii. The method

The brief gets interrogated before it gets answered. Every choice — a colour, a price, a channel — carries a reason it can defend out loud. Feedback is treated as a guardrail, not a verdict, and jargon stays at the door, because a strategy nobody in the room can repeat is a strategy nobody will fund. The method stays elastic enough to break its own rules when the market says something better, and consistent enough to get somewhere worth arriving at.
